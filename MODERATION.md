# Moderation ownership and escalation

## Ownership

- Primary: founder-designated moderation owner.
- Backup: a named, reachable backup with access limited to what the role needs.
- The names and contact paths must be filled in before public beta; do not publish personal phone numbers in the repository.

## Initial operating targets (confirm before launch)

- Urgent credible threats, exploitation, or immediate safety concerns: acknowledge promptly, escalate to the designated backup if the primary is unavailable, and follow applicable law/platform procedures.
- Routine abuse reports: triage within one business day.
- Appeals: review by someone other than the original decision-maker where feasible.

These are proposed operating targets, not a staffed service guarantee. Do not launch public registration until coverage and escalation availability are real.

## Workflow

1. Receive a report and assign a case ID.
2. Classify urgency and policy category; capture only the minimum evidence needed.
3. Apply proportionate action: warn, rate-limit, temporarily suspend, or remove access according to a documented policy.
4. Escalate urgent cases to primary and backup; document actions without unnecessary sensitive content.
5. Notify the reporter when safe and appropriate; provide an appeal path.
6. Review repeated patterns and tune controls without collecting chat content for general analytics.
7. Delete case data under the retention schedule.

## Launch blockers

- Name primary and backup owners.
- Publish user-facing rules, report path, and appeal path.
- Define severity examples, retention, access control, and applicable legal escalation procedures.
- Test an urgent escalation tabletop exercise.
